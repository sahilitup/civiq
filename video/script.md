# CIVIQ explainer video (16:9, ~55 s)

Voiceover: ElevenLabs, warm Indian-English narrator. Footage: Google Flow (Veo 3.1 Fast) for real-world shots, real app screens for the product part.

| # | Time | Picture | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–7 s | **Flow:** early morning Gurugram roadside, a garbage heap spilling onto the road next to a footpath, traffic and autos passing, handheld | Every day in Gurugram, garbage piles up on our roads, empty plots and street corners. |
| 2 | 7–14 s | **Flow:** a resident walks past the pile holding their nose, takes out a phone and makes a frustrated call | We complain. The complaint disappears. Nobody knows whose job it was, or whether anyone ever came. |
| 3 | 14–19 s | **Flow:** the same pile a week later, bigger, a stray cow eating from it, evening light | So the same spot stays dirty for weeks. |
| 4 | 19–26 s | **App:** report flow – photo, "Location locked · Ward 25" | CIVIQ changes that. Take one photo at the spot. Your location and ward are detected automatically. |
| 5 | 26–34 s | **App:** "Who must clean this" – sanitary inspector, councillor, MLA with photos | We tag the people responsible: your ward's sanitary inspector, the zone officer, your councillor and your MLA. |
| 6 | 34–41 s | **App:** public map with pins, a report page "Seen by 6 people" | Your report goes on a public map. Neighbours confirm it, and the longer it waits, the harder it is to ignore. |
| 7 | 41–49 s | **Flow:** municipal sanitation workers in uniform loading the garbage into a truck; then the same road clean | It stays open until someone at the spot photographs it clean. |
| 8 | 49–55 s | **End card:** CIVIQ logo, "See garbage? Make it someone's job." + site address | CIVIQ. See garbage? Make it someone's job. |
