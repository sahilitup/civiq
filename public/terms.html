Terms

  1. Report only real garbage spots, from the spot, with a photo you take there.
  2. Do not photograph people's faces, vehicle number plates or private property interiors. Reports showing them may be removed.
  3. Reports, photos and locations you submit are published on the public map and in open data, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.
  4. Official names and contact details are taken from public MCG sources and may be out of date. Ward boundaries on the map are approximate.
  5. CIVIQ is provided as is, without any guarantee that a report will be acted on. It is not an official MCG complaint channel; for urgent issues call the MCG helpline 1800-180-1817.
  6. We may remove reports that are not garbage, are abusive, or break these terms.
