How the numbers are counted

Reports

One report is one garbage spot photographed on the spot. A second report within 50 m of an open report is not counted again; it is added as a "seen" confirmation on the first one. Reports removed after a "Not garbage?" review are left out of every number.

Resolved

A report counts as resolved only after someone within 100 m of the spot submits a live photo and a reviewer approves it. The resolution time is the time between the report and that photo.

Resolution rate

Resolved reports divided by total reports.

Days waiting

For an area page, the typical (median) age of its open reports.

Hot spots

Places where two or more reports were filed within 50 m of each other.

Streak

The number of days in a row, ending today, on which at least one report was resolved.

Wards

MCG has 36 wards (2025). MCG publishes which localities each ward contains but not a boundary map, so ward edges here are drawn from those official locality lists using OpenStreetMap, inside MCG's outer limit. Spots near a ward edge can land in the neighbouring ward. Assembly constituencies use the official 2008 delimitation map.

Officials

Councillors, zone Joint Commissioners, Senior Sanitary Inspectors and Executive Engineers come from mcg.gov.in. Sanitary inspector and engineer offices are numbered I–VI; we match them to zones I–VI by number.
