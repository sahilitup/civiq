A public map of Gurugram's garbage, and who must clear it.

Garbage gets cleared faster when it is clear whose job it is, and when everyone can see how long it has waited. CIVIQ makes both visible.

How it works

  1. Report. Photograph a garbage spot from the spot. Your location is detected automatically.
  2. Tag who is responsible. The report is linked to its MCG ward, councillor, zone sanitary inspector, Joint Commissioner, MLA and MP.
  3. Keep it visible. Neighbours confirm it. It stays on the public map until someone standing at the spot photographs it clean, and a reviewer approves the photo.

Who is behind it

CIVIQ is a citizen project. It is not run by, or affiliated with, the Municipal Corporation of Gurugram (MCG) or any political party. Officials' names and contact details come from MCG's public website.

Ways to help

  • Report spots you see, and tap "I saw this too" on reports near you.
  • Verify cleanups when a spot near you is cleared.
  • Join or register a volunteer community.
  • Share CIVIQ with your RWA.
