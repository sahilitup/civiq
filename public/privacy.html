Privacy

Reports are anonymous. We do not ask for your name, phone or email to report garbage.

What we store when you report

  • The photo you take, the exact location of the spot, the time, and the answers you choose (problem, size, waste type, landmark). All of this is public on the map.
  • A random device ID kept in your browser, so we can stop duplicate "I saw this too" taps and limit spam to 5 reports per day. It is never shown publicly or included in data downloads.

Verifying a cleanup or saying "Not garbage?"

We store the photo and its location to check you were within 100 m of the spot. The photo carries a strip with the time, ward and coordinates so reviewers can see when and where it was taken. Reviewers sign their decisions with their name.

Weekly digest and communities

If you subscribe to the digest we store your email and the name you give, only to send the digest. If you register a community, your contact name and phone are seen only by the CIVIQ team to verify the listing.

Services we use

Map tiles come from CARTO and OpenStreetMap, place names from OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from unpkg and cdnjs. These services receive your IP address when your browser loads them. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Removal

To remove a report that shows a person, a vehicle number or anything private, use "Not garbage?" on the report, or contact us.
